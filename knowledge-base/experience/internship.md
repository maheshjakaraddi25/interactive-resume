# Internship Experience: Daimler Truck Innovation Center India

## Role: Software Engineering Intern
**Duration:** 2022 – February 2023
**Location:** Bangalore, India

---

## Overview
My internship at Daimler Truck Innovation Center India overlapped with my final year of BE at KLE Technological University. This was the same company where I later joined full-time — the internship was essentially an extended interview that turned into a job offer.

## What I Did

### Automation Workflows
I built automation workflows for microservice deployments across Azure cloud services. At the time, some deployment steps were still manual or semi-automated. My work involved scripting these steps and connecting them to the existing pipeline infrastructure.

### Terraform Expansion
I expanded existing Terraform modules to enable automated deployment of 15+ cloud resources. This was my introduction to infrastructure as code — understanding how Terraform state works, how to structure modules, and how to avoid common mistakes like hardcoding values or ignoring state file management.

This internship experience directly shaped the deeper IaC work I did as a full-time engineer.

### Production Support
I supported production deployment pipelines — monitoring deployments, debugging failures, and escalating when needed. Being exposed to production infrastructure as an intern was genuinely valuable; it built a respect for reliability that has shaped how I approach infrastructure ever since.

## What I Learned
The internship gave me a real understanding of how enterprise engineering works — the processes, the review culture, the importance of documentation, and the discipline required to operate systems that real teams depend on. It was significantly different from university projects, and that gap was important to experience early.
